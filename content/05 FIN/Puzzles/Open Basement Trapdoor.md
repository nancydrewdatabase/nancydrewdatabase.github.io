## Clues
* The trapdoor is visible, but partly covered by a wooden crate
## Unwritten Rules
* The crate covering the trapdoor is too heavy to push out of the way, but not too heavy to lift with the right tools
## Solution
* Click on the ropes 8 times until you hear a “clank”
- Attach the hook to the box
- Click on the ropes 8 times until you can’t anymore
