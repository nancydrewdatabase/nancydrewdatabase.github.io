## Prerequisites
* [[Day 2| Day 2]]
* Talk to [[Nicholas Falcone]] until he tells you about [[Eustacia Andropov]]
## Clues
* There’s a new phone number to the right of the phone after talking to [[Nicholas Falcone]]
## Unwritten Rules
* You can’t see these phone numbers for this when calling the numbers. You also can’t write down the second number at all and have to memorize it.
* Recommended write down number or have a guide open to have the phone numbers handy
* Note: Calling [[Sherman Trout|Library of Congress]] before [[Eustacia Andropov]] gives you the number unlocks [[Audiophonic Fun Menu]]
* Technically this puzzle is optional, but if you don't make the calls, the game will just think you did on [[Day 3]]
## Solution
* Call [[Eustacia Andropov]]
* Call [[Sherman Trout|Library of Congress]]
## Reward
- On [[Day 3]], a package with the [[Library of Congress Slide]] will be delivered after being told by [[Nicholas Falcone]]
