## Unwritten Rules
* The enemies will always try to get close to you, unless there is a wall in the way
* Enemies can either move one space each time you move or stay in the same place
## Solution
* There are multiple potential solutions
## Junior
Solution 1
- Move up twice, right twice, up three times, right, down twice, right four times, up, left, up and right[^1]

Solution 2
- Go up, up, right, up, up, right, up, right, down, down, right, right, right, right, up, left, up and right[^3]
## Senior 
Solution 1
- Move right, left, down, right three times, down, right, down twice, right, up, right, up, right, up, left, up and right[^1]

Solution 2
- Down twice, right, up, right twice, down, right twice, down, right, up, right, up, left, up, right[^2]

Solution 3
- Go down, right, right, right, up, left, right, down, down, right, right, right, right, up, left, up and right[^3]
