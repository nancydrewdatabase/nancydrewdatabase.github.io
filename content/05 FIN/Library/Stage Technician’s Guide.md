## Text
### The Houdini Transformation
####  *** Class 5 Authorization ***
Due to the restricted nature of this stage manipulation's usage, you will need Class 5 Technician status to perform this procedure. 
#### Control Board Operation
1. Preparation
	1. Be sure the control board Power Button is activated
	2. On the Numbered KeyPad on the control board, enter the six digit security code **(121192)** for the Houdini Transformation as listed on page 73 of your Class 5 Stage Technician's Code Book. 
	3. Press the *Left and Right Fader Power Buttons* to bring power to the *Fader Switches*
2. Maneuvering the Iron Cages
	1. When cued by the performers, lower the two iron cages to the stage floor by moving the *Left and Right Fader Switches* down to their lowest position.
	2. After the cages reach the stage floor, press the *Magnet Button* to attach the Tunnel Columns to the cage bottoms.
	3. After both performers have entered the cages, move the *Fader Switches* up slightly to raise the cages three feet above the floor. The Tunnel Columns will still be attached to the cage bottoms, although you will not see them. They are hidden by mirrors.
	4. On your cue from the performers, press the *Trap Door Button* to open the trap doors.
3. The Lock Button and Other Security Measures
		*Important ~ You must depress the Lock Switch after all stage manipulation have been completed in the proper order, as this will lock all elements in place. For security purposes, all stage elements and board controls will move to their original positions if the Lock Button is not activated.* 
		The Lock Button cannot be activated until all stage manipulation have been completed *in the proper order.*

#### The Secret Rooms & Tunnels
##### The Quick Switch Box
The performers in the cages will exchange places via the Quick Switch Box under the stage
##### The Trick Chair, Foot Elevator and Tunnels
A third performer will exchange places with the fourth performer, seated in the Trick Chair in the front row of the auditorium. To access the Trick Chair from the Quick Switch Box, the third performer will have to use the tunnel system beneath the Box. The tunnel system runs between the Quick Switch Box, to the Magician's Room, and the Trick Chair.

##### Trick Chair
The third performer can access the Trick Chair via the Foot Elevator beneath the chair. A seamless transition from the fourth performer to the third will be achieved by using a large costume/shell. The shell rests sturdily in the chair whether a performer is inside it or not.

##### The Magician's Room
This room, located beneath the Stage and beside the Basement, is for Magicians and related staff/performers only.
