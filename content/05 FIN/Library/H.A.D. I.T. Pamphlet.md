**H**UMANS **A**GAINST THE **D**ESTRUCTION OF **I**LLUSTRIOUS **T**HEATERS

Make no mistake: these old theaters are at the top of the architectural endangered species list: and once they're extinct, we'll never be able to bring them back.

Humans Against the Destruction of Illustrious Theaters was founded in St. Louis in 1997 to rally public attention for the cause of preserving unique historical theaters throughout the  nation. As of today, HAD IT has been instrumental in saving 133 theaters and the organization boasts 37 chapters nationwide. But even as the movement continues to expand, the need for greater public participation has never been greater. Every day theaters that have served generations of audiences are being bulldozed to make way for gigantic megaplexes and shopping malls.
