## Text
### Monitoring the film and the projector:
- Focus Knob

*Once the film is properly loaded (see p. 77 for full instructions), adjust the focus knob to clarify the picture. Different film will come into focus at different points*

*While film is running, stay beside the projector and continue to monitor the following:*
- The loops are correct
- All rollers are rotating properly
- The surface of the film is not being scratched or obstructed
- The sprocket holes are not being strained or torn
- The film is winding safely and smoothly on the take-up reel.

_*If the images on the screen begins to flicker or the sound becomes warped, or any of the above processes begin to fail, switch off the projector immediately._

**NEVER leave the projector running unattended.**

### Cleaning and Maintenance:
*Regular cleaning of your projector will keep it running well and prevent built-up dirt from scratching the film.*

*Use a toothbrush to remove built-up and a paintbrush to remove dust from the film pathway. Apply methlated spirits to a soft cloth and use this to wipe the film path.*

_*During screenings, dust the film pathway with a clean paintbrush after each reel of film_
