### **The Phantom Message**

#### The Effect:
Set a sharpened pencil and a small sheet of paper on your magician's stand and select a volunteer from the audience. After turning your back, ask the volunteer to write a message on the sheet of paper with the pencil.

When she says that she is ready, you pick up the sheet of paper while making a point not to look at it. Crumble the paper into a ball, and to the amazement of the audience, make it explode into a ball of flame!

You then produce a deck of cards from your pocket and set it on your magician's stand. The volunteer picks a random card from the deck.

Explain to the volunteer that her written message has risen from the ashes and will make itself known! Take the sharpened pencil and begin to rub the face of the volunteer's card gently with the side of the pencil tip.

Within seconds, words begin to appear on the card. It's the exact message the volunteer had written on the sheet of paper!

#### The Secret:
This trick will utilize a few of the techniques and gimmicks that we have covered in previous chapters. 

First, as you place the pencil and paper on the stand, secretly have a playing card (face up) hidden beneath the sheet of paper. As the volunteer writes her message on the paper, the pencil will leave an imprint of the message on the playing card. (Note: Be sure your pencil is sharp!) After the volunteer is finished, lift the paper and the card at the same time with your right hand, while keeping the card hidden. Take the piece of paper with your left hand and secretly leave the card in your right hand using the *palming* technique from chapter three. As you crumple the paper in your left hand, you will secretly be busy with your right hand:

Place your right hand into your right jacket, release the card and slide your *flash paper trigger* onto your index finger. Then secretly palm a wad of *flash paper* as you remove your hand from your jacket pocket. (See "Flash Paper and Other Effects" in chapter seven.)

While holding the crumpled message in your hand, (and hiding the flash paper in your right hand,) give the illusion that you're taking the crumpled message into your right hand. This will be achieved by using the *French drop* technique from chapter two. As you pretend to hold the message in your right hand, secretly drop the real message into your left jacket pocket with your left hand. 

Now, in your right hand, ignite the flash paper with a spark from your flash paper trigger as you throw the wad of flash paper into the air. It will burst into a very quick and very bright ball of flame. *(Important notice to young magicians: Use flash paper only under supervision of an adult)*

With your left hand, pull a deck of cards out of your inside pocket and set them on your magician's stand. 

As you do this, slip your right hand into your right jacket pocket, secretly remove the flash paper trigger from your index finger, and take the card (with the imprint on it) into your right hand. Palm the card from view as you take your right hand out of your pocket.

Secretly put the imprinted card into the deck of cards on your magician's stand and make sure that *this card* will be chosen by your volunteer. This can be achieved by using a *card force*, as outlined in the next chapter.

After the volunteer chooses the imprinted card at "random", gently rub the side of the pencil tip across the face of the card. The imprint of the message will surface, amid the applause of your audience.

### Chapter Nine: The Card Force
As you undoubtedly learning, nothing is really left to chance in magic. When you ask someone to choose a "random card" from deck of playing cards, one thing is for sure: He will always take the card you want him to take. How? By using a card force.
#### The Automatic Tenth
Though very simple, this card force is quite effective. Before you perform the trick, secretly set it up by doing the following: Take a deck of cards and place it face-down on a table. Take a card of your choice (let's say, the Queen of Hearts) and place it as the *tenth card down from the top of the deck.*

Hand this secretly stacked deck to the audience member. Place it face-down in her left hand and tell her that she is going to choose a random card out of the deck.

Ask her to *choose a number between 11 and 20*. (For our purposes, let's say she picks "14".) You then ask her to count and place, from the top of the deck and one at a time, fourteen cards into a *new pile on the table.*

After she has done this, ask her to *take this new pile* and place, it facedown in her left hand.

Now, ask her to *add together the two digits that comprise her number.* (Since she has picked 14, the digits would be "1" and "4". Therefore: 1 + 4 equals "5".)

Then, ask her to count and place 5 cards, one at a time, off the top of the deck and *into a new pile on the table*. When she is done, ask her to *turn over the top card on this new pile*. This is the "random" card she has chosen. It also just happens to be the Queen of Hearts!

How does it work? The magic is in this trick happens through a mathematical equation. Choosing *any* number between 11 and 20 and adding those two digits together will always automatically reveal your tenth card! Try it!

You can use this force in conjunction with The Phantom Message trick on page 56. By leaving a stack of nine cards in your right jacket pocket, dropping it carefully to the bottom of this stack will automatically make it the tenth card! You can then palm the entire stack into your hand and secretly place it on top of the deck of cards on your magician's stand.
