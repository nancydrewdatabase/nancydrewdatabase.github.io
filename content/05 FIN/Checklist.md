## Speedrun
- Based on [Yandema211 run in 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N0821six0eQ)
- This is one of the shortest Nancy Drew games to speedrun, would recommend for beginners!
- Speedruns here are determined by three main factors
	- Puzzles
	- Navigation
	- Dialogue
### Before the speedrun
- In the settings, disable player and character dialogue
