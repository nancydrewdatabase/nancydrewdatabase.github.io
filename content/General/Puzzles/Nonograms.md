Nonograms go by a number of different names. 
- Nonogram, named after one of the creators Non Ishida 
- Picross, a portmanteau of Picture Crossword, is trademarked by Nintendo
- Griddlers was named primarily for UK newspapers and puzzle books
- Plus many other variations! They are all the same puzzle.

Nonograms are picture logic puzzles with numbers on the top and left side to determine which squares of the grid should be filled in. TODO: details on how to play
