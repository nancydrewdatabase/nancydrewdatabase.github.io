There's a whole subreddit of games that remind people of Nancy Drew called [r/NancyDrewRevival](https://www.reddit.com/r/NancyDrewRevival/). I'm going to shout out some of my favorites mystery games that may or may not be a recreation of Nancy Drew but I want to talk about anyways.

## Penelope Pendrick
I have been following the creator [[Default Cube Games]] since her recreation of TRT in an open world format. This feels like a true return to form of the older games, including the navigation (which can be a major factor for some people, I personally hate moving the camera when I'm playing a game). It's more than the puzzles and characters, the atmosphere is really nailed and is a good game overall. Looking forward to more in the series as the first one was amazing.
