Renograms, named after [[Rentaro]] is actually based on the puzzle Hidato or Hidoku. This logic based number puzzle has a goal of filling a grid with consecutive numbers where the boxes connect horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Unlike [[Sudoku]], the grid is not necessarily square or rectangular. The boxes themselves can be squares or hexagons. 

Renograms are kind of the inverse of a connect the dots, where you know what the number is supposed to be, the question is where to place it.
