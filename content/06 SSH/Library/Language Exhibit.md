### Maya Language

The Maya writing system is the most advanced ever developed in the New World. As the Egyptian hieroglyphics, symbols represent either sounds or concepts. Mayan is considered a distinct linguistic group, and several modern variants exist today, including Quiche, Cakchiquel, and Ixil.
