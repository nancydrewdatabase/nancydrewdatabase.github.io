#### bul

#### atanzahab
- Matchmaker, hired by a boy/groom's parents to assess the suitability of a prospective marriage. (Bride and groom were often married without ever having spoken a word to each other.) Check to see if there's an exhibit on this for the Temple Quiz.
#### GREENSTONE
- Jade, jadeite, serpentine...all diff. shades of green Maya considered jade to be divine - far more important than gold. Used it often to make both artistic and sacred objects. The six keys would likely be carved out of this??

#### LAMANAI
- City's name means 'submerged crocodile'. Crocodile = depicted in many glyphs
#### paal kaka
- The priest, responsible for giving children their first names.
#### NAMING
- Prefixes: 'ah' for boys; 'Ix' for girls.
- After prefix, the name of a mammal, reptile, or bird (eg. Boy: Ah Balam; Girl: Ix Cuat)
- After puberty, the child would take on his or her father's last name, as well.
