### <u>King Pacal Carving</u>
- Content: Ancient king of Palenque
- Material: Jade
- Age: Classic Period (c. 650 CE)
- Identifying Marks: 
	- Glyph: combination of Pakal shield glyph, the "Lord" glyph, and the "Imix" (one) disk on the forehead.
	- Translation: The First True King
- Dimensions: 4.0 x 4.0 inches
- Weight: 0.43 lbs

**Provenance:**
<u>TIMELINE</u>
- Site of excavation ***UNKNOWN***
- year of excavation: *unknown*

- First known record of ownership:
	- 1940, given by Rupert Starr to the Serpentine Gallery in Los Angeles, California (8290 Wilshire Blvd., Los Angeles, CA 90010)
- 1955, sold by Serpentine to Felix and Miranda Peterson, Connecticut.
- 1993, sold at auction by Peterson estate to Beech Hill Museum, Washington, D.C.

### <u>TRANSFER OF TITLE: PACAL CARVING</u>

May 4, 1940

### Bertrum Vandalay Attorney at Law

December 2, 1992

Dear Taylor:

As you may have heard, my longtime clients, Felix and Miranda Peterson, both passed away this summer in the heatwave. Felix was 89 and Miranda was 91, and by all accounts they had lived extraordinarily full lives. Still, it's too bad their air conditioning went on the blink when it did. I and all citizens of Greenwich will miss them dearly.

As you know, the Petersons had no children. Per their instructions, I am to arrange the auction of their estate and donate the proceeds to charity. As you may also know, the Petersons had a fabulous art collection, including several pre-Columbian artifacts that may be of particular interest to you. The auction will be held on February 7th.

Please let me know if you will be planning to attend.

Sincerely,
Your friend and colleague,
Bertrum Vandalay
