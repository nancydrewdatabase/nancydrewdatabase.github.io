## Text

7/16/72: hiked with Big Bunny all day. Temperatures in 100's, water scarce, but highland region is beautiful and pristine (except for Bunny's incessant commentary).

even spotted a quetzal flying amongst the branches. A rare treat! Symbolizes freedom and wealth. Tail highly prized by the Maya.

7/18: hard rain last night, shared tent w/ Big Bunny out of kindness (pity). What does he eat? Nearly suffocated.

7/22: crossed paths with old shaman today. Eager for some relief from Big Bunny, accepted old man's invitation to eat. Old man (Vicente) gave us very strong tea to which BB reacted negatively. He kept shouting "bunny rabbits, flaming bunny rabbits!" (Return of the repressed?) Once he even pounced on the floor to try to catch one, but there was nothing there. Vicente thought this was very funny. I nearly expired from embarrassment. 

Tea had no unusual effect on me. A very amused Vicente. Yaaargh!

Old man told us legend has it that the highlands are haunted by the spirit of Amoxcalli. (I understood most, translated for Bunny). Here is the legend as best I could gather:

At the height of Pacal's reign, a royal Maya scribe <u>Cihuapilli Amoxcalli,</u> wrote an account of the Maya that somehow defamed Pacal's name. Amoxcalli disappeared, along with all traces of her work. The rumor was that Pacal banished the scribe and her writings to the far reaches of the netherworld. Highland elders swear that Amoxcalli has haunted these hills for centuries and will not rest until her writings are unearthed and her name is restored to <u>dignity</u>. 

"Banished"? hah! If only Pacal were so lenient...

Would have enjoyed the story much more without the unwanted <u>"outbursts"</u> of my foolish sidekick. Quite a tale though, amazing that it has survived somehow over hundreds of years.

7/23: Big Bunny wants to start a small-time $muggling racket to pay off his college tuition. Ugh, he's the last person I'd want for a partner in crime, but I do need some way to fund my own studies.

I wonder if I could stand it...

7/29: Travels today have been uneventful. Sun is hotter than ever. Tomorrow we return to base camp. Cannot wait to be rid of my companion (suspect he is really a dog in bunny clothing!).
