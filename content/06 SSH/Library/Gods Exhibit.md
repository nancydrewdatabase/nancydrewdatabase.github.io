### Maya Gods
Religion played an important role in Maya society. All scientific progress was impelled by the priests' need to understand the manage the sacred forces of the Maya universe. Different gods represented aspects of Maya life, from basket weaving to calendar days. Even today, little is known about the role or names of the gods represented in Maya artifacts.

### Maya Gods
The three masks depict the corn god, Yum Kaax, the jaguar god, Xbalanque, and the sun god. Ahau Kin.
