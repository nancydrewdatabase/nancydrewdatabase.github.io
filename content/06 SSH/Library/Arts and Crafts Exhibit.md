### Maya Arts and Crafts
The Maya were renowned for their functional and stylistic expertise in pottery, painting, architecture, and jewelry. Artisans worked with a variety of materials, including clay, jade, gold, limestone, and wood. The variety of tools and materials used by the Maya demonstrate a complex and thriving trade system. 

### Offering Bowl
Ceramic, Muyil
Post Classic Period, CA. 850 C.E.

Offering bowl to the war god, Balac. The logograph for 'black' is represented on the base of the bowl.

"ek" = black
