## Text

Congratulations on your purchase of the state-of-the-art Dyna-Ham Model 2000! We at Kremercom know that you will enjoy your new communication device for years and years. Below are some helpful pieces of information for both your safety and convenience.

And remember, if it isn't a **KREMERCOM**, it's *asymptotic*! 

### The Wonderful World of Ham Radio
Your new Dyna-Ham Model 2000 lets you listen in on parts of the world you never knew existed and interact with friends from faraway lands! The space-age digital keypad allows you to enter a 4-digit frequency or channel. You can then either listen to the activity on the channel or use the Dyna-Ham Morse Code Keyboard to transmit a message. Please consult local, state, federal, and international laws on appropriate and legal usage.
### Receiving
To enter in a frequency or channel number, simply enter a 4-digit number on the numeric keypad and press the **CONNECT** button.
### Transmitting
All messages are sent using Morse Code (see Appendix) one letter at a time. Compose each letter by pressing either a **DOT** or **DASH** button on the Morse Code Keyboard. When you have finished composing a letter, press **SEND**.

The "<" button erases the series of **DOT**s and **DASH**es that have been entered prior to hitting the SEND button. 

If you realize you have made a mistake after hitting the **SEND** button (one or more times), pushing the "<<" button will send an "ignore previous message" signal.

If you have transmitted an unrecognizable letter, a "transmission error" sound will play.
### Maintenance
Good maintenance techniques will extend the life of your Dyna-Ham 2000 Model. Avoid exposure to liquids, solvents, bodily fluids and caustic chemicals. If the main radio tube has expired, it can be replaced with any standard radio tube.

CAUTION: Avoid placing fingers or foreign objects into the main radio tube holding socket; electrical shock can result.

### Morse Code Alphabet
In Morse Code, letters are composed of a combination of dots and dashes. For example, to send out the word **CAT**, one would enter -.-. for 'C', .- for 'A' and - for 'T'.
