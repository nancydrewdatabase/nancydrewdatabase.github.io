## Prerequisites
* The first tile in the museum garden
* The second tile in the display case
## Clues

## Unwritten Rules
* There will be hints of using specific glyphs that look like heads and others that use a more straightforward numbering system. The solution will have a mix of both throughout.
## Solution
* Use the dots for 1s and bars for 5s. Then use clues from [[Sonny Joon's Floppy Drive]] to determine the remaining head glyphs.
