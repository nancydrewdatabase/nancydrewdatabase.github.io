## Clues
* [[Sonny Joon's Journal]] 
* Pascal's Triangle
	* Specifically shows the probability of each number you can roll
	* Great explanation from [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/nancydrew/comments/rt6d3o/comment/hqrdask/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=web3x&utm_name=web3xcss&utm_term=1&utm_content=share_button) that I will try to simplify the probability
		* 2 is most common (37.5%)
		* 1 or 3 is second common (25%)
		* 0 or 4 is least common (6.25%)
## Unwritten Rules
* You have to play both sides as the player and the computer
* Each roll will let you move between 1 (one dot) to 5 spaces (zero dots)
* You may pass the second roll of your turn, can be strategic 
* You win a round if you end on the same spot as your opponent
* Your goal is for orange to win 4 times
## Solution
- Based on Pascal's Triangle, 1, 2, or 3 are the most common rolls so you can plan accordingly
* Honestly, just roll/pass for both sides until you win
