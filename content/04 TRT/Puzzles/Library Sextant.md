## Prerequisites
* "[[Enter Wickford Library (Through the Vents)]]"
## Clues
* _A sly rabbit will have three openings to its den_
	* Possibly meaning there are 3 numbers to input
* [[Library Globe]]
## Unwritten Rules
* Use the top red button to select
- Use the center green button to reset
- Back away from fireplace puzzle in the bottom right
## Solution
* Click above the fireplace and input the same numbers as the clue inside the globe
- -15, 10, -5

## Reward
- Opens a secret room on the second floor of the library
