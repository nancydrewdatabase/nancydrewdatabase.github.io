## Prerequisites
* [[Enter the Basement Gate]]
## Clues
* The image on the other side of the wall shows the height each chain
## Unwritten Rules
* Have to redo this puzzle each time entering the [[Wickford Royal Tower]], but the solution is the same every time
* Back away to reset the puzzle
- Each chain can move multiple other chains

|Chain 1|Pulls 1 and 4|
|---|---|
|Chain 2|Pulls 1 and 2|
|Chain 3|Pulls 2 and 3|
|Chain 4|Pulls 4 only|
|Chain 4|Pulls 1 and 5|
## Solution
* Pull Chain 5 twice
- Pull Chain 3 one
- Pull Chain 2 twice
- Pull Chain 1 once
- Pull Chain 4 twice
