## Prerequisites
* [[Chain Puzzle to Cross the Bridge]]
## Unwritten Rules
* Coins will always move all the way across unless they hit the edge, a fence, or another coin
- Strategically move the coins to make barriers for other coins
## Solution
### Junior Solution[^1]
- Step 1
	- Pink Snake - left, up
	- Blue Dog - down, left, up
	- **Yellow Leopard** - down, left, up, left
- Step 2
	- **Blue Dog** - right, down 
- Step 3
	- **Pink Snake** - right, down, left
### Senior Solution 1[^1]
- Step 1
	- Pink Snake - left
	- Gray Eagle - up, left, up
	- Blue Dog - left
	- **Yellow Leopard** - up, left, down, left 
- Step 2
	- Pink Snake - up
	- Gray Eagle - down, right, up, left, up
	- Blue Dog - up, right
	- Gray Eagle - down, left, up
	- **Blue Dog** - left, down
 - Step 3
	- Gray Eagle - right, up
	- **Pink Snake** - down, right, up, right, up, left, down 
### Senior Solution 2[^2]
 - Part 1
	- Yellow Leopard - up, left
	- Pink Snake - left, up
	- Gray Eagle - left, up
	- **Blue Dog** - right, up, left, down left, down
-  Part 2
	- Gray Eagle - right, up, right, down
	- Yellow Leopard - right
	- Pink Snake - down, right, up
	- Gray Eagle - up, right 
- Part 3
	- Yellow Leopard - down
	- Pink Snake - right
	- **Yellow Leopard** - up, left, down 
- Part 4
	- Pink Snake - down
	- Gray Eagle - right and up
	- **Pink Snake** - up and left
	- **Gray Eagle** - down, left, up
