## Prerequisites
* [[Enter Wickford Library (Through the Door)]]
* Set [[Wickford Elevator]] on [[Wickford Second Floor]] or [[Wickford First Floor]]
* [[Oil Can]]
## Clues
* Noticeably blocked trap door when [[Enter Wickford Library (Through the Vents)]]
## Unwritten Rules
* After [[Enter Wickford Library (Through the Vents)]], you have to do the same puzzle in reverse to to [[Enter Wickford Library (Through the Door)]] to have the [[Wickford Elevator]] in the right spot not to block the trapdoor
## Solution
* Use the [[Oil Can]] on the trap door
