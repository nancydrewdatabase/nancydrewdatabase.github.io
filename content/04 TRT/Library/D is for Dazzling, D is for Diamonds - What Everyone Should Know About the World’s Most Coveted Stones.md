### The 5 C's of Diamond Appraisal
Color, Cut, Clarity, Carat... and Cost.
#### Color
Diamonds are clear, right? Not so! Actually, when viewed under a special jeweler's light, you can see that diamonds come in every color of the rainbow. What's more, the color of the diamond is the second biggest factor in its price, after carat weight. The clearer, or more colorless, the diamond is, the more it's worth. Most diamonds have at least a hint of yellow or brown body color. Others, called "fancy" diamonds, have a blue, pink, purple, or red tint. If a diamond does not have enough color to be called fancy, then it is graded in a scale of colors ranging from "D" through "Z". A "D" diamond is considered to be colorless. At the other end of the spectrum, anything more intense than "Z" is considered fancy. A fancy yellow diamond is more expensive than a light yellow diamond.

#### Cut
Diamonds come in a variety of shapes: Round, Emerald, Heart, Marquise, Oval, Pear, and Princess. Still, not every diamond of the same shape looks the same. Two heart-shaped diamonds can appear very different, actually, and you don't need to be a diamond expert to see that one may have more "shape appeal" than the other. The most popular shape that diamonds are cut into is the "round." It is also the most brilliant, (or sparkly!) of all the cuts. The "Ideal Cut" for a diamond is the one for which diamond proportions achieve the most brilliance.
